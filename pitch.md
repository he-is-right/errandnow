ErrandNow — Coming Soon

ErrandNow is an AI-enabled errand-hailing marketplace designed to make everyday tasks easier, safer, and more convenient. The platform will connect individuals and businesses with verified service providers for deliveries, personal shopping, personalized local market errands, domestic tasks, personalized requests, and business support services, all through one easy-to-use mobile platform.

Customers will be able to request errands, track them in real time, make secure digital payments, and access reliable assistance with confidence, just like booking a ride.

ErrandNow is currently in development and is being spearheaded by Bigaz Innovations, the technology division of Bigaz Network International Limited.
